01 / THE IDEA

A better standard for beauty bookings.

FairPlay brings a standardised framework for fairer and clearer booking, helping providers communicate their policies transparently while keeping control over what works for their business.

01

Faireness through clarity

Fairness starts with knowing what you’re agreeing to. FairPlay helps make booking terms, requirements and expectations clear before either side commits.

02

Provider autonomy

Fairness doesn’t mean every provider has to operate the same way. FairPlay gives providers the flexibility to set policies that work for their business within a transparent framework.

03

Easier booking

Less back-and-forth. FairPlay brings booking, availability and appointment management into one simpler experience.
